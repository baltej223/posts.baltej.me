# Welcome to posts.baltej.me — My Personal Publishing Hub

Hey there! 👋

This is the very first post on **posts.baltej.me**, my custom-built publishing platform, designed, coded, and crafted from scratch by me.

Here, I’ll share my thoughts, projects, tech explorations, and whatever sparks my curiosity. Unlike typical blogs, this site runs on a unique static site generator that I built myself, giving me full control over how content is created, stored, and displayed.

There will be a clear boundary between my blogs and this website. On my blog, I’ll share structured, in-depth articles and long-form content focused on specific topics, learning journeys, and tutorials.

You can expect MDX-powered posts, and a sleek reading experience and It’s all powered by JSON files and smart indexing behind the scenes.
